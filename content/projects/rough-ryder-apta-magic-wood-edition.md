This slipjoint from Rough Ryder is such a cool design. And they are remarkably cheap! They punch way above their weight in terms of quality and design. I love customizing these. I was practicing my "groot" or "wood" texture. It is done with a cutter on the dremel. I tried to make it look like it was magical wood with some crazy energy inside of it waiting to burst forth. I was inspired by Tasha Suri's Burning Kingdoms series.
