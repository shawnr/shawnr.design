These Chaves Fat Man drivers were super fun to work with. They are so perfectly machined and balanced. The thick barrel gives me a lot of torque. This is an item where I did one for a friend and kept one for myself. The spinner top is the icing on the cake, too -- super fidgety and fun. I kind of threw everything at this -- it has some sparkle, some foil, black ano, and color ano.
