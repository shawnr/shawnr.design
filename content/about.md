I make things. I anodize titanium, etch copper and brass, force patinas, engrave, swap parts, and otherwise customize knives, pens, coins, skill toys, and all sorts of objects. I have also been known to build hobby electronics, make physical artwork, and create strange devices. This site is a collection of all those things I've been able to photograph, video record, or otherwise document from the real world.

I sell my work on my [Etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/ShawnRiderDesigns). You can find readymade pieces that are finished and ship right away, as well as stock pieces that can be customized to your desire — pick your colors, patterns, and finishes and I'll make it just for you.

Please browse and enjoy this archive of projects. If you have any questions you can reach out to me on social media at the links below.
