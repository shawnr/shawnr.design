This Deadbolt bit driver / bottle opener from TKell Knives is a really beautiful item. It features milling in the style of the Dia de los Muertos sugar skulls. I had to amplify that effect on one side with color highlights on a black base. On the other side I did a faux-flamed finish and made the linework black.
