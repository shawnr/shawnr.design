This Dreamtech flipper was so fun. It had a cool milled pattern on the main body of the show side scale, so I wanted to make the most of that. I also wanted to practice my "cracked ice" texture, so I did that on the bolsters and top part of the blade. I feel like it came out really nice. The shimmer is outrageous!
