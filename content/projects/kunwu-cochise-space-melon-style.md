This Kunwu Cochise is already an incredible knife: The "honeydew" texture is so exotic, and the shape of the blade is so brutal and modern. This one already had timascus collar pivots, so it was a looker from the beginning. I polished up the scales and applied a two layer set of anodizations: First high voltage colors in the background. Then I re-sanded, re-polished, and brushed lower voltage colors to make it look as trippy as possible. I think I did a good job.
