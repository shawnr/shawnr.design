On this Chaves Ultramar I really went for it with the cracked ice finish on the blade itself. I think it looks amazing. It features a pattern in green and purple on the show side. On the clip side it has a feature texture that catches and reflects the light. The clip is just straight-up GREEN. This was a super fun one to work on.
