This run of 60 titanium pens from D Rocket Designs was truly epic. It took three weeks to crank through these. I think some of the best work I've done went onto these pens. The models are the Suppressor and the Pocket Pen. They are all titanium bodies, but other than that they are very different style pens! I used every technique I know on these. I'm super proud of how they came out.
